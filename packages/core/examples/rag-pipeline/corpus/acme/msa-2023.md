# Master Services Agreement — Acme Corp
Effective 2023-01-01. tenant: acme | doc_type: msa

## 7. Service Levels
Provider targets 99.5% monthly uptime. Where Provider fails to meet the target, Customer is
entitled to an SLA credit against the following month's fees.

## 7.3 Severity 1 breach
For a Severity 1 breach, the SLA credit is **10% of monthly fees**.
