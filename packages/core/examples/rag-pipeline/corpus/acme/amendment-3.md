# Amendment 3 to the Acme MSA
Effective 2025-04-01. tenant: acme | doc_type: amendment

## Revision to service level rebates
The service level rebate payable on a Priority One incident is increased to **25% of monthly
fees**, superseding the rate stated in clause 7.3 of the Master Services Agreement.

This revision was requested by the Customer following the outage of 2025-02.
